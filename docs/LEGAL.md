# 3EVO Router — Legal & Provenance Note

## Provider list

The bundled provider list (`src/data/providers.json`) was derived from public
router implementations. If you are the author of a listed provider and want your
entry removed or corrected, open an issue.

## Subscription token routing

3EVO Router is a local proxy. Routing subscription API keys through a third-party
gateway may violate the upstream vendor's terms of service and can result in
account suspension or bans.

This project does not endorse or facilitate unauthorized access to paid services.
Users are solely responsible for complying with each provider's terms.
